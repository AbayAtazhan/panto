# Panto — press kit

**One-liner:** Panto is a free, open-source macOS screen recorder that turns clicks and hotkeys
into smooth camera zooms.

**Paragraph:** Screen Studio costs $89, and most people only use it for one thing — zooms on the
parts of a demo that matter. Panto records any display at native resolution with ScreenCaptureKit,
captures system audio and microphone as separate tracks, and bakes a clamped, smoothed camera
move into the mp4 afterwards. A timeline editor lets you add, drag and delete zooms before export.

**Facts**
- macOS 15+, Apple silicon and Intel
- Swift 6, no Electron, no third-party dependencies
- MIT license
- No accounts, no telemetry, no network requests
- 1.6 MB DMG, ~15 MB installed
- Not notarized yet: first launch needs right-click → Open, or the one-line installer
